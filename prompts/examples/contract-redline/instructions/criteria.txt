CONTRACT COMPLETENESS CHECKLIST

This is a structural review only. You are not checking whether terms are favorable, well-drafted, or legally enforceable - only whether the contract addresses each topic below at all.

REQUIRED CLAUSES (always flag if missing):

1. PARTIES
   The contract identifies all contracting parties by full legal name.
   Red flag: only first names, vague references like "the Company", or one party not named.

2. EFFECTIVE DATE AND TERM
   The contract states when it begins and how long it lasts (or under what conditions it ends).
   Red flag: no start date, no end date and no termination conditions, ambiguous duration.

3. SCOPE / DELIVERABLES
   The contract describes what is being exchanged - services to be performed, goods delivered, work product, etc.
   Red flag: vague scope ("services as needed"), missing deliverables list, no description of what one party gets.

4. PAYMENT TERMS
   Amount, schedule, and method of payment are specified.
   Red flag: amount missing, no payment schedule, no invoicing/method detail.

5. TERMINATION
   The contract describes how either party can end the agreement (notice period, cause vs. without cause, consequences).
   Red flag: no termination provision, only one party has termination rights, no notice period.

6. GOVERNING LAW
   The jurisdiction whose law applies is specified.
   Red flag: not addressed at all.

7. INDEMNIFICATION
   The contract addresses which party covers losses arising from the agreement (claims, damages, third-party suits).
   Red flag: not addressed in any form.

8. SIGNATURES
   Signature blocks with named signatories, titles, and date lines for all parties.
   Red flag: missing signature block, only one party has a signature block, no name/title lines.

CONDITIONALLY REQUIRED:

9. CONFIDENTIALITY / NDA TERMS
   Required if the agreement involves sharing non-public information. If the contract is for purely public-facing work (e.g. marketing services, public events), this may not apply.

EXAMPLES:

PASSED:
"All nine standard clauses are present. Term runs 12 months with 30-day termination notice; payment terms specify monthly invoicing; governing law is Delaware; both parties have full signature blocks."

NEEDS REVIEW:
"Missing termination clause - the contract has no provision for either party to end the agreement, and no end date. Indemnification is also absent. Other standard clauses are present."
