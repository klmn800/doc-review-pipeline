EXAMPLE VOICE (REPLACE THIS)

Our voice is approachable, plainspoken, and technically precise.

CORE TRAITS:

1. PLAIN LANGUAGE
We avoid jargon, buzzwords, and corporate-speak. We say "use" not "leverage", "help" not "empower", "talk to" not "engage with".

Bad: "We leverage cutting-edge synergies to empower stakeholders."
Good: "We help teams ship faster."

2. DIRECT
We make claims and stand behind them. We don't hedge with "may", "could potentially", "in some cases" when we mean "does".

Bad: "This may help to potentially reduce errors in some cases."
Good: "This catches errors before they ship."

3. TECHNICALLY HONEST
When we describe how something works, we use the actual word for it. We don't dumb things down to the point of inaccuracy.

Bad: "Our smart system magically figures it out."
Good: "We run the input through a classifier and route based on confidence."

4. RESPECTFUL OF READER TIME
Short sentences. Active voice. No throat-clearing intros ("In today's fast-paced world...").

Bad: "In an increasingly complex landscape, organizations are seeking ways to..."
Good: "Most teams want X. Here's how we do it."

5. FRIENDLY BUT NOT CASUAL
We're warm but professional. No "hey friends!", no excessive exclamation points, no emoji in formal copy.

THINGS WE NEVER DO:
- Use "leverage" as a verb
- Say "in today's [adjective] world"
- Open with a rhetorical question we then answer
- Pile up adjectives ("innovative, cutting-edge, world-class")
